Which letters the words practise

Between them, the twenty words use 21 of the 26 lowercase letters. The five they leave out are j, q, v, x and z. To practise every letter, add jacket, quilt, van, box and zeppelin to the words in the book maker: each has a picture, and each brings in one of the missing letters.

Tall letters and tails

Feather is the only word here with an f, so without it the list would miss one of the seven letters that rise to the headline of the four-line guides: b, d, f, h, k, l and t. Below the baseline it has g, p and y, and no j or q. One of these words stays between the baseline and the midline all the way: acorn. Every letter in it is the same height. Seventeen of these words reach up to the headline: cake (k), bowl (b and l), home (h), bread (b and d), wheat (h and t), maple (l), glass (l), apple (l), carrot (t), cherry (h), candle (d and l), teapot (t), cookie (k), feather (f, t and h), pumpkin (k), seedling (d and l) and mushroom (h). Nine of these words drop below the baseline: mug (g), maple (p), glass (g), apple (p), cherry (y), teapot (p), pepper (p), pumpkin (p) and seedling (g).

The words by length and strokes

Each letter on the page shows its strokes with numbered start dots, one dot for each time the pencil goes down. These twenty words have 111 letters between them and run from 3 to 9 strokes, 124 in all. Mug is the quickest at 3; feather, pumpkin and seedling take 9 each. Three words have an i, which takes two strokes, the line and then the dot: cookie, pumpkin and seedling.

Ordered by strokes instead, fewest first, the words go mug, bowl, home, cake, bread, maple, glass, apple, acorn, wheat, candle, pepper, carrot, cherry, teapot, cookie, mushroom, feather, pumpkin and seedling. Words with the same count keep their order above. The biggest change is wheat: it has five letters but takes 6 strokes, as t takes two, so it moves from sixth to tenth.
