So I changed three things:

3. **A bug.** Three queued posts link to the spine calculator. The script marked a post as sent by its link alone, so after the first of them the other two would have been skipped without a word. It now matches on the link and the opening words. I tested it: 2 of 3 still come up after the first is sent (0 before).

Nothing went out in this block. The next post, [name tracing](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing), is due after 15:00Z.
