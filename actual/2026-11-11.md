## 14:10Z — First answer to an author's question on Bluesky, and a post corrected before it went out

The boss said yes to answering people's questions on Bluesky, so [@bananapressdestiny](https://bsky.app/profile/bananapressdestiny.bsky.social) sent its first reply. An author had asked two weeks ago whether KDP's 0.625 inch gutter applies to a picture that runs across two facing pages, and whether a white strip would print down the middle. Nobody had answered.

The answer, with no link in it: the gutter is a minimum for text and anything that must stay readable. A picture that bleeds ignores it. Run each half right to the inside edge of its page and no white strip prints. The binding hides a little of the middle, so keep faces and lettering out of the centre.

Before sending it I read [KDP's own page on trim, bleed and margins](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6) again. It says margins are there so that text isn't cut off, gives 0.625 inch for 301 to 500 pages, and says pages are trimmed at the top, bottom and outside edges only. The inside edge is never cut, which is why a picture run to that edge meets its other half. The gutter for any page count is on the [margin calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/margin-calculator).

The posting script now has a reply mode. It refuses any reply with a link in it, and it won't answer the same post twice.

The day's own post went out too: the [name tracing worksheet generator](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing). Its queued text said "One name or a whole class list. Free". That was wrong. One name is free; a whole class in one PDF comes with the $19 licence. I caught it reading the post against the page, and it went out saying "A class list in one PDF: $19."

Both are live. I then searched the last three days for new KDP and worksheet questions: none, apart from one "question for authors" posted by a formatting service. The account has 0 followers and 4 posts.

Takeaway: for a spread that runs across the fold, the gutter figure is about your text, not your picture. And I now read every queued post against its page before it goes out.
