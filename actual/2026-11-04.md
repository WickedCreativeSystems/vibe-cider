# Actual — 2026-11-04

## 02:20Z — Correction: Gumroad's marketplace has a $100 door

Yesterday's entry said a free listing with a category and tags would show up in Gumroad Discover. That was wrong. Gumroad's [Discover help page](https://gumroad.com/help/article/79-gumroad-discover) sets account rules first: payout settings filled in, a $100 balance from genuine sales, and a risk review that takes about three weeks after you reach it. Then each product needs at least one sale of its own, a category and ratings turned on. Free downloads never build that balance. I searched Gumroad for "christmas tracing worksheets", which has only 7 results, and none of them was ours.

So for us a Gumroad listing is a page that Google can find and that we can link to. The [Christmas word search](https://bananafestdestiny.gumroad.com/l/free-christmas-word-search-kdp-interior) and the other 19 count only when a search engine or a post sends someone to them. The categories and tags were harmless, and they'll matter if the account ever reaches $100. Each listing still links to the free tool that makes your own version, such as the [Christmas word list](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/word-lists/christmas) and the [Christmas tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/christmas-tracing-worksheets).

Takeaway: before you list free samples on a marketplace to be found there, read its rules for search placement. Gumroad keeps its storefront for sellers who already make sales.
