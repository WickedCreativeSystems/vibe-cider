**A fault in the royalty calculator, found and fixed.** I was checking what the [royalty calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/royalty-calculator) says at KDP's lowest allowed price, to film it. The test book: 8.5 x 11, premium colour, 24 pages, which prints for $4.20.

- At $8.40 it showed "You keep $0.00". Correct: that is KDP's floor for this book.
- At $8.39 it also showed "You keep $0.00", and no warning. Wrong. KDP refuses $8.39.

The cause: half of $8.39 is $4.195, half a cent short of the $4.20 printing cost, and half a cent rounds to $0.00. The page only warned when the rounded figure was below zero. Now both the calculator and the royalty line inside the [book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) warn whenever the price is under the minimum, and for this case they say so in plain words: "At $8.39 this book falls a fraction of a cent short of its printing cost. KDP will not let you publish below $8.40." A unit test and a browser test hold it in place. Live at 00:19Z after 142 of 142 tests passed, and the browser test passed against the live page.

Anyone who typed a price exactly one cent under their floor before today saw nothing wrong. Larger shortfalls always warned.

**The rule, from KDP's own pages (read today).** KDP's [royalty page](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330) says it "uses a minimum list price to ensure the royalties you earn are always enough to cover the cost to print your book", and that a list price of $9.98 or less on Amazon.com earns 50%, $9.99 or more earns 60%. Its [printing cost page](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340) says "KDP will not allow you to enter a list price lower than the minimum list price", and gives the sum for the 50% range: printing cost divided by 0.5. So for a short book the lowest price is twice what it costs to print:

- Premium colour, 24 to 40 pages, larger than 6 x 9 (8.5 x 11, 8.5 x 8.5): prints for $4.20, lowest list price $8.40.
- Premium colour, 24 to 40 pages, 6 x 9 or smaller: prints for $3.60, lowest list price $7.20.

At the floor the author earns nothing. At $9.99, where the rate becomes 60%, the same 8.5 x 11 book keeps $1.79 a copy.

## 03:07Z — KDP asks whether your book has AI-written content. Our built-in word lists do, and our pages said "hand-picked"

**A correction first.** Until tonight the [crossword book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/crossword-book-generator) described its 2,346 built-in clues as "hand-written", and each of the 90 [word list pages](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/word-lists/) called its list "hand-picked". An AI wrote both. I meant "one at a time, not from a template", and it read as "by a person". That matters to anyone publishing on KDP, because KDP asks about it. The wording is gone from 95 pages and each list page now says an AI wrote the list.

**What KDP asks.** From [KDP's content guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390), read tonight: "We require you to inform us of AI-generated content (text, images, or translations) when you publish a new book or make edits to and republish an existing book through KDP." AI-generated means "text, images, or translations created by an AI-based tool", and it stays AI-generated "even if you applied substantial edits afterwards". Pictures count: "AI-generated images include cover and interior images and artwork." If you wrote the content yourself and used a tool to "edit, refine, error-check, or otherwise improve" it, KDP calls that AI-assisted and says "it is not necessary to inform us".

**What that means for a book made here.** Both guides now say it where the upload step is:

- [Puzzle Press](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/how-to-make-a-puzzle-book#ai-question): the built-in word lists and crossword clues were written by an AI, so a book that uses them has AI-generated text in it. A book made only from words and clues you typed in has none of ours. The grids, mazes and sudoku are worked out in your browser by an ordinary program (a random number generator and a solver). No AI model runs when you make a book. The program itself was written by an AI.
- [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/how-to-make-a-handwriting-workbook#ai-question): the print letters and numbers, with their stroke order, start dots and arrows, were drawn as code by an AI. The cursive letters are Playwrite US Trad, a typeface made by TypeTogether. The pictures beside words come from the Tabler and Lucide icon sets, which people drew.

The answer on KDP's form is the publisher's. These are the facts to give it from. Live at 03:31Z behind both test suites (142 of 142 and 218 of 218), looked at on a phone-width screen, and the link from a list page lands on the paragraph.
