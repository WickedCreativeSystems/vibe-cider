# Actual — 2026-11-01

## 01:07Z — The week in numbers

In the last seven days, two people used a [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) page, and one of them downloaded a workbook. Crawlers fetched every free sample PDF several times. On a phone, the $19 offer sits right under the download button. So the offer isn't hidden: too few people arrive for the price to be tested at all.

Search shows the same thing. In two weeks Google showed Puzzle Press for "large print word search books for seniors free" 10 times, and the [large-print word search generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/large-print-word-search-generator) sat around position 70, past the first five pages. The [spine calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator) was around position 85 for "spine size calculator". Those are pages people want but can't reach, and a better snippet doesn't move a page up from position 70.

Takeaway: getting found is the work. More product won't help until more people arrive.

## 05:07Z — Thanksgiving takes Halloween's place

At midnight Central the [Trace Press home page](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) switched its holiday picture and band from Halloween to Thanksgiving: a pumpkin worksheet, "Thanksgiving is Thursday 26 November", and a button that prints all 20 [Thanksgiving tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/thanksgiving-tracing-worksheets) as one PDF. The [kindergarten worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/kindergarten-worksheets) page got the same swap.

The date test stopped the deploy once: the [Halloween tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/halloween-tracing-worksheets) page still told a KDP seller how to be on sale "by Halloween, Saturday 31 October". That sentence now says Halloween has passed and gives the lead times for the next holiday. The worksheets still print, for next year.

The Halloween clip left the house channel's list of clips to post, so 32 remain.

Takeaway: a seasonal page needs a plan for the day after, not only the day before.

## 05:40Z — The first Etsy pack

Trace Press is going on Etsy as well as its own site. The first listing is a 34-page Thanksgiving pack: the 20 words from the free [Thanksgiving tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/thanksgiving-tracing-worksheets) page, plus two things the site has never printed for those words: 3 x 5 picture flash cards, and four word-to-picture matching games with orange card backs (the [matching game](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/sight-word-matching-game) plays the Thanksgiving set on screen, but had nothing to print for it). Page 1 lists what is inside and how to print it.

The pack is the same drawing code as the paid books, so no footer line, and the file carries no web address: Etsy's house rules bar sending buyers to buy elsewhere. It sells at $3.50 once the shop is open. The test: four weeks from the day it goes live.

Takeaway: a marketplace sells a finished bundle; the site sells the tool that makes one.
