# Actual — 2026-11-02

## 00:07Z — One book, three spines

A 48-second clip of the free [spine calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator) is now in the media index for the docent. It shows one 300-page 6 x 9 paperback switched between three papers. On white the spine is 0.676 inch and the full cover 12.926 inches wide. On groundwood they are 0.705 and 12.955 inches. On cream they are 0.750 and 13.000 inches. The clip ends on KDP's own warning: a cream book over 350 pages, or a white one over 525, needs a new cover if you switch paper. KDP's help pages give no groundwood thickness. The calculator uses the figure from KDP's own cover tool, 0.00235 inch a page.

Takeaway: the paper is part of the cover size, so pick it before you design the cover.

## 02:07Z — The Christmas date, counted the same way twice

Two of our sites gave different last days for the same KDP rule. KDP says a low-content book's review is taking three to four weeks. Four weeks back from 25 December is Friday 27 November, and that is what [Trace Press's Christmas worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/christmas-tracing-worksheets) said. Three Puzzle Press pages said 20 November, a week earlier with no reason given. The [home page](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/), the [large-print word search generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/large-print-word-search-generator) and the [Christmas word list](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/word-lists/christmas) now say 27 November and show the four-week count. Earlier is still safer: KDP's Timelines page can change, so check it on the day you publish.

Takeaway: a deadline you publish should show its arithmetic, so a reader can check it and so can you.
