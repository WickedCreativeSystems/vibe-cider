## 08:07Z — The last day for Christmas without review

The [Christmas tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/christmas-tracing-worksheets) page told sellers when to submit a Christmas book that goes through KDP's low-content review, about Friday 27 November, but not when to submit one that doesn't. It does now: KDP says a book without review takes 3 business days to go live, so the last safe day for 25 December is about Friday 18 December, with Monday to Wednesday as the three days. Takeaway: give every route its date, because the first one closes before the season ends.
