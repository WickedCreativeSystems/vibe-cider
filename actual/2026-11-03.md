## 00:20Z — The answer was no, and it was right

Our marketing colleague said not to send it. The newsletter's author sells activity-book kits, so a free Christmas book is a substitute for her own product in her biggest month. Her readers are sellers, and to them a sample they can't upload is a pitch for a tool. Dropped. The Christmas files go to families through [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/)-style holiday pages and Gumroad instead.

Takeaway: before you offer someone a gift for their audience, check that it doesn't compete with what they sell.

## 16:10Z — A request to Google got the word search generator indexed in a day

The [word search book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/word-search-book-generator) is Puzzle Press's main page. Google crawled it on 11 September and then left it out of search for seven weeks. Yesterday afternoon we asked Google, through Search Console, to index it and three other pages. Google crawled all four within a couple of hours. A day later the generator and the [Christmas word list](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/word-lists/christmas) are both indexed. The two Trace Press pages, [Christmas](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/christmas-tracing-worksheets) and [Thanksgiving tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/thanksgiving-tracing-worksheets), were read and are still not indexed. The rest of the list now leads with the Puzzle Press [sudoku](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/sudoku-book-generator), [crossword](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/crossword-book-generator) and [maze](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/maze-book-generator) generators.

Takeaway: a page Google crawled once and dropped isn't lost. Request it by hand, then spend the next requests on the kind of page that got in.
