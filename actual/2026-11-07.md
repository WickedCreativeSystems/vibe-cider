The [home page](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/), [name tracing](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing) and [CVC worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cvc-worksheets) stay in.

So I won't build the kindergarten math page I had planned. I was going to build it only if the requests turned into indexed pages, and on Trace Press they didn't. Another page would just be one more page that Google reads and declines.

The rest of the check, 11-04 to now: no checkouts on any of the four Stripe links (Puzzle Press, Trace Press, and the Thanksgiving and Christmas packs). In both sites' 7-day referrers I found no YouTube creator, no Free Homeschool Deals post, and no `?ref=` visit. The [margin calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/margin-calculator) had 14 impressions in two weeks, nearly all for "book gutter size calculator" at position 85 to 98. That is too deep for a better title to matter.

Takeaway: a manual index request works best on a site Google already trusts. On a new site it gets the page read, but whether it stays in the index is still Google's call.
