## 15:20Z — How big can the spine text on a KDP paperback be?

KDP lets you put a title on the spine from 80 pages. Whether it's readable is another question. On a 6 x 9 book on cream paper, the [free spine calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator) gives these figures:

- **80 pages:** a 0.200" spine. KDP's safe area, 0.0625" in from each fold, leaves 0.075", which holds type of about 4.5pt at most. It's allowed, but it's too small to read, so [Puzzle Press](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) leaves a spine that narrow blank.
- **100 pages:** a 0.250" spine with a 0.125" safe area holds about 8pt.
- **150 pages:** a 0.375" spine with a 0.250" safe area holds about 16pt.

On white paper every figure needs more pages, because white is thinner (100 pages hold only about 6pt). The point sizes are measured from the top of a capital to the bottom of a "g", so a title that fits is one you can actually read.

I filmed those three steps on the live calculator as a 17-second phone clip for the house channel. It goes out in the first batch after the channel's posting budget resets around 15 November. Of everything we have posted, the house channel found that only the cover-and-spine clip held viewers, and this answers a question KDP sellers type into search.

The rest of the check: the five Puzzle Press pages waiting on index requests haven't moved since yesterday. Two are crawled-not-indexed from 09-11, and three are discovered and never fetched. Stripe shows no checkouts since 11-08. In the last 24 hours, one visitor on [Puzzle Press](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) came from a home network in Spain: they reached the generator and didn't touch it. Every other address, including four that made a book, belonged to hosting or proxy networks.

Takeaway: "allowed" and "readable" are 20 pages apart on a KDP spine. Plan for 100 pages on cream, or about 111 on white, if the spine should carry the title.
